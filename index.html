CurShip — Shopify delivery app

A shipping app for South African Shopify stores. A merchant sees what an order costs to ship, books a courier, prints the label and follows the parcel without leaving Shopify.

It works with real Shopify orders: fulfilment, labels, and cancel with refund, tested in a development store. Courier booking is tested in The Courier Guy's sandbox, and the wallet runs in test mode. It isn't launched to merchants yet.

About me

A bit about
who I am

I'm Anotidaishe (Richard) Mazhindu, a web developer based in Pretoria, South Africa. I build Shopify stores and standalone websites for clients and my own brands. I also design ads and email campaigns, and build web applications with React, Node.js and Supabase.

I started teaching myself web development while I was still in high school at Hudson Ntsanwisi Senior Secondary School. After matric in 2025, I turned it into client work, trading as AZHYRE.

I'm available for web development roles, contracts and freelance projects.
